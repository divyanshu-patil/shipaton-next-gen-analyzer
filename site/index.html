About the Next Gen Award

Who: active students (high school, university, bootcamp, academic programs) with a qualifying student email. Ages 13+; minors need parent or guardian consent.

What to submit: a demo video (under 2 minutes, on YouTube or Vimeo) and a public, open-source code repo with a license that shows in the repo's About section. No store release or paid developer account needed.

Judging criteria:

  • Is the idea clear, useful, interesting or original? Does it solve a real problem?
  • Meaningful progress toward a working app, with core functionality clear from the video and repo
  • Thoughtful use of RevenueCat (subscriptions, IAP, web purchases, ads…)
  • Thoughtful technical choices, product thinking, and care in how it's built and presented

Prizes: 1st $20,000 + Shippy trophy + Times Square billboard + 9to5Mac/9to5Google spotlight + App Growth Annual invite · 2nd $10,000 · 3rd $5,000

Timeline: submissions closed Sep 30, 2026 · judging Oct 1–13 · winners announced Oct 21 · Times Square Demo Day Oct 22
